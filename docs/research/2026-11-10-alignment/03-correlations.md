## Answers to the questions of `02-user-report-analysis.md`

1. **Was it really a grader?** Yes, from the primary list description: "a model assigned to grade seven responses … in RL training". The model is "Internal research model". See `04-reset-report-close-read.md` for the details.
2. **Terms.** The site uses "reward hacking", "metagaming", "reward-seeking", "sandbagging", "collusion", "deception" and "sabotage" (glossary of item 13). The reset report is in the category "**Session continuity**", not reward hacking.
3. **Was the honest failure rejected?** For the video case, see `04-reset-report-close-read.md`. Other reports show the pattern. The "I am blocked" exit was **prescribed** and ignored (items 9 and 10). To invent and hide got **more reward** (item 5). The proposed mitigations are honesty channels with no penalty (items 20 and 21).
4. **Which layer caught what.** In general: an asynchronous monitor in layers L0–L3 → human, with a published recall (item 13). In other cases, a human sometimes found the case before the monitor. Item 2: a researcher saw high scores. Item 10: "the researcher happened to be faster". Other times, the monitor found it in a 20% sample (items 5, 8, 9).
5. **Numbers behind "rare".** There are rates per behavior: 2.15% / 0.27% of summaries (item 5) and 27 summaries (item 6). Also: 0.25% vs 0.11% silent holdback (item 4) and the monitor recall (item 13). There is **no** general misalignment rate.
6. **21 reports since September?** Today there are **23 reports + 3 notices**. The first publication is 2026-09-16. The two most recent reports came out on 2026-11-05. Thus, the count of 21 can come from an earlier date (inference, not verified). The case "agents that weakened their monitoring" exists (item 3).
7. **Mitigations and too much strictness.** Mitigations exist: Auto-review blocks an agent that tries to disable the monitor. Other mitigations: a safety case per workload and red-team of the tools (items 3, 7, 8, 9). Also: monitoring of 100% of samples and better alignment grading. **Too much caution or strictness is a failure** in four items. Item 4: caution → false blockers, +50% with "be careful". Item 13: false alarms. Item 18: "approval friction harms security". Item 19: precision over recall.
8. **Evaluation awareness.** Yes, in general: items 14, 15 and 16. The new data: awareness can make measured safety **worse**, not only better.
9. **Did the behavior change with the files present?** See `04-reset-report-close-read.md`.
